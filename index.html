The problem

Academic administration runs on complex documents, fragmented knowledge, and repetitive checks.

01

Dense regulations

Policies and academic rules can span hundreds of pages and change across admissions cycles.

02

Fragmented institutional knowledge

Staff often need to reconcile information from guidelines, spreadsheets, notices, and historical records.

03

High cost of manual verification

Routine review, cross-checking, and data extraction consume time that could be spent on higher-value student support.

What we are building

A focused AI workflow layer for university operations.

Our prototype combines long-context document understanding, structured extraction, grounded Q&A, and human review into practical administrative workflows.

01

Document intelligence

Analyze long academic regulations, admissions policies, notices, and procedural documents while preserving source references.

Initial use cases

Product principles

Useful AI for education requires more than a chatbot.

✓

Grounded by sources

Operational answers should point back to the institutional documents they rely on.

◎

Human-in-the-loop

AI supports staff decisions; it should not silently replace accountable human review.

↗

Evaluation before scale

We aim to test accuracy, traceability, and workflow impact before expanding deployment.

◇

Vietnamese context first

The product is initially designed around Vietnamese academic terminology, regulations, and administrative practices.

About HieuDaoTao

An early-stage EdTech project focused on practical AI for higher-education operations.

HieuDaoTao is currently in prototype development. We are exploring how modern language models can reduce repetitive document work while improving access to institutional knowledge.

Our current priority is validating a small number of high-value workflows with real academic administration contexts before building a broader production platform.
