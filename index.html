The problem

Academic administration runs on interconnected regulations, fragmented documents, and manual verification.

Higher-education operations cannot rely on generic chatbots or unverifiable summaries. Every operational interpretation carries institutional responsibility.

01 / DENSE REGULATIONS

Interconnected regulations

Policies, decrees, and training regulations span hundreds of pages across multiple academic years, creating high cognitive load for staff.

02 / FRAGMENTED SOURCES

Fragmented policy documents

Guidelines, admissions circulars, procedural notices, and amendments are scattered across departments, spreadsheets, and file drives.

03 / MANUAL OVERHEAD

Costly manual cross-checking

Staff expend hours searching manually to reconcile conflicting clauses, verify exceptions, and cross-reference conditions before advising students.

04 / TRACEABILITY GAP

Difficult source traceability

Informal advice or generic AI tools lack citations back to authoritative clauses, making answers risky to rely on for administrative actions.

Why Claude

Technical fit for long-form academic reasoning and verifiable workflows.

HieuDaoTao is designed with a provider-agnostic reasoning layer. We are particularly interested in evaluating Claude for policy-intensive higher-education workflows where long-document reasoning, grounded outputs, structured tool use, and reliable human review are critical.

Long-document reasoning

Academic regulations often contain interconnected requirements distributed across many sections, appendices, and related circulars. Large context windows allow evaluating the complete document without losing cross-article coherence.

Grounded structured outputs

Administrative workflows need outputs that strictly preserve source references and can be transformed into structured review schemas for reliable auditing.

Tool-enabled workflows

Claude can serve as an analytical reasoning layer connecting document retrieval, structured extraction, cross-referencing validation logic, and human review handoff.

Evaluation approach

How we evaluate prototype reliability & grounding.

Before considering broader deployment, every workflow must undergo rigorous validation across four fundamental dimensions.

01 / DIMENSION

Answer correctness

Does the output correctly interpret the source regulation without hallucination or distortion of administrative intent?

02 / DIMENSION

Citation accuracy

Do statements point strictly to the authoritative articles, clauses, and appendices, enabling rapid verification by staff?

03 / DIMENSION

Extraction consistency

Are extracted fields, tables, and conditions predictable, schema-compliant, and reproducible across varying document versions?

04 / DIMENSION

Human review efficiency

Can academic staff verify outputs faster while retaining accountability and complete decision authority?

The prototype has completed an initial end-to-end live validation using a sample Vietnamese academic regulation, including server-side verification of supporting evidence. Broader evaluation across real institutional documents is the next step.
Evaluation protocols are designed for collaborative pilot trials. No arbitrary benchmark percentages are claimed.
